## Applicability by deployment model

Community and public-read deployments must preserve the public-surface
privacy controls, non-accusatory language standard, source-quality caveats,
and human-review requirements before any high-risk allegation is acted on.

Private or reviewer-only deployments may expose additional investigative
context, but they still inherit the same data-source limitations, abuse
response process, security disclosure path, and legal disclaimer.

## Change log policy

Material legal, privacy, security, abuse-response, or ethics changes must be
recorded in `CHANGELOG.md` under `Unreleased` and reviewed before release.
