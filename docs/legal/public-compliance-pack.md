# Public Compliance Pack

This file summarizes the public-read guardrails validated by repository gates.

## Public Surface

Public routes must avoid raw personal document fields, avoid public graph/demo
payloads with person or partner nodes, and expose corruption patterns as
reviewable signals rather than factual accusations.

## Required Controls

- Public pattern evidence must be source-backed and auditable.
- Public outputs must preserve identity-confidence and source-quality caveats.
- High-risk interpretation requires human review before action.
- Abuse, vulnerability, and privacy requests follow the root policy files
  linked from the legal index.
